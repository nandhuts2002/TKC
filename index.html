Cardamom Processing

From Field to Finished Grade, All Within the Plantation

At TKC Plantations, cardamom is processed entirely within the plantation using a carefully managed, step-by-step process. From washing and drying to polishing, grading and selective sorting, each stage is designed to maintain the natural colour, aroma and quality of the spice.

  1. 01

    Washing

    Freshly harvested cardamom arriving from the field is first fed into a washing machine. The pods are thoroughly washed to remove soil, mud and other field impurities.

  2. 02

    Heat Pump Drying & Dehumidification

    The washed cardamom moves to the drying unit, where a heat-pump dryer removes moisture through controlled dehydration and dehumidification. Drying runs for a specified period to preserve the natural colour, aroma and quality.

  3. 03

    Polishing

    Once dry, the cardamom moves directly to the polishing machine, which removes flower remnants and other dry residues attached to the pods.

  4. 04

    Size Grading

    The polished cardamom passes through a grading machine and is separated into size categories.

    • 5 mm
    • 6 mm
    • 7 mm
    • 8 mm
    • 8.5 mm
  5. 05

    Selective Sortex Cleaning

    After grading, the cardamom is sent to the Sortex machine, which identifies and removes pods with visible blemishes, colour deficiencies and other quality defects.

What Makes Our Process Different

Graded first, then selectively sorted

In the conventional method described, cardamom is commonly passed through the Sortex machine in bulk before size grading. At TKC Plantations the sequence is different: cardamom is graded first and then sent for selective Sortex cleaning. This allows higher-value grades and cardamom intended for export orders to receive additional optical sorting and cleaning.
